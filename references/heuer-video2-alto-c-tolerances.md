## 1. Alto C (#3 C) Core Measurements

In Chris Heuer’s sizing chart, **#3 C** corresponds to an **Alto C** ocarina[cite: 1]. The **Window Size** refers specifically to the distance from the **airway exit** to the **labium lip**, accounting for ~12% clay shrinkage between the wet and fired stages[cite: 1].

> **Correction (2026-11-17, from a frame of the board).** The board uses ×1.06 (6 % shrinkage) for six of the seven sizes; only the #3 C row is ×1.126. The "~12 %" and "add 12 %" below generalise that one row, and the Est. Wet column below uses it. The fired values are unaffected. The window-size definition (exit to labium) is not confirmed from the video itself. See docs/01-source-review.md §5.

| Measurement | Original (Inches) | Metric (mm) | Maker's Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Airway Exit Height** | `.045"`[cite: 1] | **1.14 mm** | ~1.5x the thickness of a standard credit card (`.03"` / **0.76 mm**)[cite: 1]. |
| **Window Size (Wet)** | `.428"`[cite: 1] | **10.87 mm** | Target measurement while shaping wet clay (includes shrinkage allowance)[cite: 1]. |
| **Window Size (Fired)** | `.38"`[cite: 1] | **9.65 mm** | Final target distance from airway exit to labium lip after firing[cite: 1]. |

## 2. Alto #3 C Volume & Tolerance Scale

Tiny variations in the **fired window size** directly dictate the breath pressure required and the overall volume of the instrument[cite: 1]. *(When shaping wet clay, add approximately **12%** to the fired dimensions).*[cite: 1]

| Rating / Volume | Original (Inches) | Fired Size (mm) | Est. Wet Size (mm) | Acoustic & Playing Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **X (Too Small)** | `.32"`[cite: 1] | **8.13 mm** | ~9.15 mm | Out of tolerance; unlikely to play well or reach a full octave[cite: 1]. |
| **Soft** | `.35"`[cite: 1] | **8.89 mm** | ~10.01 mm | Requires gentle breath pressure; produces a quieter tone[cite: 1]. |
| **Medium (Ideal)** | `.38"`[cite: 1] | **9.65 mm** | **10.87 mm** | Balanced breath pressure and ideal medium volume[cite: 1]. |
| **Loud** | `.41"`[cite: 1] | **10.41 mm** | ~11.73 mm | Requires stronger breath pressure; produces a louder tone[cite: 1]. |
| **X (Too Large)** | `.44"`[cite: 1] | **11.18 mm** | ~12.59 mm | Out of tolerance; notes easily overblow or fail to sound properly[cite: 1]. |

## 3. Practical Notes for 12-Hole Alto C Makers

* **Range Precision:** Because a **12-hole Alto C** covers an extended range (up to 21 chromatic notes), keeping the fired window length at **9.65 mm**—and strictly within the **8.89 mm to 10.41 mm** tolerance band—is essential for stable high notes[cite: 1].
* **Sensitivity:** Adjusting the window length by just **0.76 mm** (`.03"`, the thickness of one credit card) shifts the voicing from a balanced **Medium** volume to either **Soft** or **Loud**[cite: 1].
