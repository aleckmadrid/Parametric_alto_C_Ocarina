## 1. Series Overview & Core Theoretical Principles

Across the series, Heuer emphasizes that ocarina voicing is an exact acoustic science governed by proportional scaling. Key governing parameters include:
* **Proportional Scaling:** As internal chamber volume decreases, the window size, airway exit height, and labium edge distance must scale down proportionally to maintain proper air jet velocity and edge tone generation.
* **Clay Shrinkage Factor:** Raw wet clay dimensions must be scaled up by roughly **12%** to hit the intended target geometry after kiln firing.
* **The Credit Card Benchmark:** Heuer uses a standard credit card (~`0.03 inches` or **0.76 mm**) as a physical shop-floor reference gauge for measuring thin windways and fine adjustments.

## 2. Breakdown by Video Chapter

### Video #1: Introduction & Basic Concepts
* **Focus:** Overview of ocarina mechanics, Helmholtz resonance principles, and chamber geometry.
* **Theory:** Explains how an ocarina functions as a Helmholtz resonator where pitch is determined by the ratio of internal volume to the total area of open fingerholes.

### Video #2: Tolerances & Sizing Standards
* **Focus:** Master sizing chart, window length tolerances, and volume scaling.
* **Parametric Takeaways:** Connects chamber sizes (#1 through #7) to exact airway exit heights and window lengths, demonstrating how a deviation of just `0.03 inches` (`0.76 mm`) transitions an instrument from *Medium* volume to *Soft* or *Loud*.

### Video #3: Voicing Tools & Techniques
* **Focus:** Crafting the voicing and cutting the labium.
* **Parametric Takeaways:** Details the physical blade angles and fipple sticks used to establish clean bevels. The labium angle must be precisely tuned so the split air jet cleanly hits the sharp edge rather than diffusing into the chamber.

### Video #4: Forming the Body & Pinch Pots
* **Focus:** Construction methods for hollow ceramic bodies.
* **Parametric Takeaways:** Teaches wall-thickness consistency. Uneven wall thickness causes uneven drying shrinkage, warping the voicing alignment and throwing off internal resonance.

### Video #5: Teardrop-Shaped Windows
* **Focus:** Advanced window geometry for extended range instruments.
* **Parametric Takeaways:** Demonstrates how shaping the voicing window into a tapered teardrop profile allows 12-hole ocarinas to achieve a stable upper register (up to 21 chromatic notes) without collapsing high-note clarity.

### Video #6: Slab-Built 5-Hole Ocarinas
* **Focus:** Simplified geometry for classroom and beginner builds.
* **Parametric Takeaways:** Applies scaled-down volume rules to smaller multi-chamber or simple cross-fingered layouts.

### Video #7: Tenor Sweet Potato Ocarinas
* **Focus:** Building larger sweet-potato style tenor instruments.
* **Parametric Takeaways:** Scales up the internal volume requirements, showing how larger chambers require wider airway exits and longer window spans to prevent overblowing.

### Video #8: Tuning & Finishing
* **Focus:** Fine-tuning pitch, carving fingerholes, and post-tuning adjustments.
* **Parametric Takeaways:** Explains hole-diameter sizing mathematics: small incremental increases in fingerhole diameter exponentially shift pitch, requiring gradual reaming rather than aggressive cutting.
